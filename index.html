How Blood Radar works

A simple way for donors and hospitals to coordinate.

🩸

Join the network

Register as a donor or hospital and add the details needed to coordinate a request.

🔔

Get relevant alerts

Hospitals can post a blood request. Matching available donors receive an in-app notification.

📍

Coordinate nearby

Donors can optionally share their location so hospitals can identify nearby matching donors.
